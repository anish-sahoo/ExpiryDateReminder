## Full description

Replaces the current one. Sits under 4000 characters.

```
Expiry Date Reminder keeps track of what's about to go off, so you find out before
you open the fridge and not after.

SCAN INSTEAD OF TYPING
Point your camera at the printed date on a label and the app reads it for you. On
phones that support it, an on-device model handles the awkward formats that
packaging likes to use. Nothing leaves your phone.

KNOW WHAT NEEDS ATTENTION
Everything is grouped by how urgent it is. Expired items come first, then whatever
runs out in the next couple of weeks, then the rest. A count at the top tells you
where you stand at a glance.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
Add the widget and see what expires next without opening anything. It changes what
it shows depending on how big you make it, from a single count up to a full dated
list.

REMINDERS THAT SUIT YOU
Choose how many days of warning you want and what time of day the reminder arrives.
Turn them off entirely if you would rather just check yourself.

MORE THAN GROCERIES
Medicine, warranties, passports, insurance renewals. Anything with a date on it.
Sort items into the built-in categories or make your own.

ALSO IN THIS VERSION
• A photo and notes on any item
• Search across everything you have saved
• Date formats for the way you write them, picked from your region automatically
• Available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish and
  Portuguese
• Colors that follow your wallpaper on Android 12 and later

Everything is stored on your phone. No account, no sign-in, and the app asks for
the camera only when you scan.
```

## Notes

- "On phones that support it" is doing real work in the scan paragraph. The
  Gemini Nano path is gated on device support and falls back to plain text
  recognition, so promising it outright would be a claim the app cannot keep on
  most hardware.
- The translation list matches `values-*` in the app. Those strings are machine
  translated and have not had a native speaker over them.
